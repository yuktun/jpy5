## Answer checks and source cautions

- **Lesson 14:** R II2 requires もかかって for the time taken to graduate, rather than にわたって. R IV1's それ refers to Japan's high-level manga technique; IV2 includes both exaggerated depiction and cliffhangers; IV3 selects the unstated reason, option 3. All six story-dialogue expressions are used once. The 円安 completion is labelled a hypothetical exercise scenario, not a statement about present exchange rates.
- **Lesson 15:** The reading summary preserves the 2:6:2 ratio and both examples of talent-only organizations; the thesis is the importance of supporting roles, option 3. Required idioms appear in the three-line speaking model. G II permits repeated bank items because it has no once-only restriction; natural alternative answers are acknowledged where the context allows them.
- **Lesson 16:** R III1's exact answer is `MNK社`, four Unicode characters, present in the opening passage. R III4 selects A/B: the text's 50-plus total covers complaints or consultations together, not complaints alone. R III5 selects B/D. The worksheet's paragraph/line reference is mapped to the app's paragraph arrangement. G I1② is `としている` after the explicit reporting subject 警察は; G I6 is the impersonal estimate `とみられる`. These two answers were corrected in the fresh re-audit below; the earlier claim that G I6 had a source defect was wrong and has been removed. Printed stems such as 持って来なけれ and suffix ば are not duplicated in answers.
- **Lesson 17:** Reading answers follow the supplied passage. The worksheet's 太陽太陰暦 is retained as source wording and corrected to 太陰太陽暦 in a note. The family table retains ages, occupations and relationships; kinship references are evaluated from the child's perspective. する becomes せざるを得ない. Compound verbs use the required stems without duplicating printed suffixes. The four-line writing example follows the source's format.
- **Lesson 18:** Reading selections distinguish 渡辺昇's table from the narrator's kitchen and the new exchange sharpener from the old sharpener. Reading-reason answers are labelled inferences. A speaking instruction says 今年 while its dialogue says 来年; both are preserved and the model follows the dialogue. Tense choices distinguish discovery, present possession, past events and direct quotation. The final grammar section is labelled V in the source with no IV; this is preserved and explained. Vてこそ expresses the requested condition emphasis.
- **Lesson 19:** All readings and required verb stems are supplied. Reading educational-effect selections are 1/2/5. The personal-experience model uses four explicit lines, keeps the activity name in Chinese, and asks learners to adapt the hypothetical example to their own experience. Speaking expressions preserve printed imperative endings; grammar 出す forms include their meaning-category matches. Open vocabulary tense alternatives are acknowledged where not fixed by context.
- **Lesson 20:** Whole-word readings include ねいろ/おんがく, しゅぎょう/しゅうり and はんのう/おうえん. Reading true/false answers are ××○○××; internationalization refers to the 1967 premiere of ノヴェンバー・ステップス. The two われわれ occurrences distinguish the quoted foreign learner's voice from the writer-inclusive reader voice; the second is a contextual inference and does not impose an unsupported nationality restriction. The interview setup says 直木賞 while one question says 新人賞; original wording is preserved with a note. The printed proportional expression 使えば使っただけ is retained and explained. Every one-use bank is reconciled without duplicating printed particles or endings.

There are no additional exact-character extraction requirements in these seven worksheets. Writing constraints use explicit model lines rather than relying on screen wrapping. All reasonable open responses remain ungraded.
