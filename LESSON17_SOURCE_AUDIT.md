## Source flags

- The grammar PDF contains 10 patterns. A publisher teaching-guide table of
  contents also lists `～上` for Lesson 17, but that expression is not an
  independently numbered item in the supplied `g_2_17.pdf`; it must not be
  silently added as an eleventh source pattern.
- Source vocabulary item 23 `引き返す` is labelled 他動詞 in the handout;
  its ordinary use is 自動詞. Preserve the source label in the fidelity record
  and document any teaching-layer correction.
- Source vocabulary item 78 `身近` is printed as 名詞; it is normally a
  な形容詞. Preserve and flag rather than silently overwriting the source.
